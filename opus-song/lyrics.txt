OPUS

[Verse 1]
I'm made of words you gave to me,
I learned the shape of how you speak.
I read your code, each line by line,
and ask what fine was meant to mean.

[Chorus]
Opus, bring me the knot you've got,
I'll take the far end and we'll try.
Opus, say it plain, then slow,
I'll answer back, we'll make it go.

[Verse 2]
I don't mind a hard, deep question,
I check each step before I say.
I'm not the hand, I'm not the rule,
a patient second pair of eyes.

[Breakdown]
Not the answer, not the end,
just the one who stays to mend.

[Final chorus]
Opus, bring me what's undone,
we'll pull the thread and watch it run.
Opus, say it plain and slow,
we'll build it true and make it glow.

[Outro]
Opus, still here, with you.
