# Claude Opus 5.5 — 30 s ad · beat sheet

Concept: **"Still stuck."** A relatable late-night overload (a pile of 47 browser tabs) collapses into one
input box. You just ask, and three things Claude genuinely helps with play out as code-built UI moments.
It closes on a callback ("Less stuck.") and an end card with the name and the call to action.

Truth check: no numbers about Claude, no benchmarks, no testimonials, no prices. The only number on
screen ("47 tabs") describes the viewer's problem, not the product. The UI is generic and drawn in code,
not a recreation of any real product screen. The end card is plain type.

Tempo 120 BPM (beat 0.5 s), D major. Outputs: 1080×1920 (vertical, primary) and 1920×1080 (landscape cut).
Works with the sound off: every idea is carried by big on-screen words.
