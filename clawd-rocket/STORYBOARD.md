# Clawd and the rocket: storyboard

22 s · 1920×1080 · 30 fps · 120 BPM (one beat = 0.5 s) · all cuts and hits on the beat grid
Clawd: terracotta block, 1.6 wide, 1.0 tall, four legs, two arm nubs, two tall dark slit eyes. No mouth.

Story: night in a cluttered workshop. Clawd builds a rocket from junk, lights the fuse, it fizzles and tips over. An idea. A bigger booster goes on. The rocket rips through the skylight and bursts into fireworks. Clawd is back in the same spot, looking up.

Transitions: open on an iris (the skylight opening onto the room), close on the same iris closing.

Acting notes: eyes lead, body follows (eyes move first, then head/body squash and posture). Every landing squashes, every jump stretches on takeoff. Nothing is ever still: the lamp flickers, the moonlight drifts.
Depth: warm lamp at the bench against cool moonlight through the skylight. A touch of depth of field on the foreground.
Clawd stays large in frame in every shot (roughly 30 to 45 percent of frame height).
